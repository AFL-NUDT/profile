01 / Profile

Research with a clear purpose.

Wu Hanshuo, PhD

Research Associate · National University of Defense Technology

I am a researcher specializing in high-power fiber lasers and fiber amplifiers, with expertise in power scaling, transverse mode instability (TMI) suppression, and novel fiber structure design.

My work addresses the fundamental limitations of high-power fiber laser systems. I develop confined-doped fiber designs, low-NA architectures, and novel pumping strategies to achieve high-brightness, high-power laser output with excellent beam quality.

03 / Research

From mechanism to system.

Connecting physical insight, fiber architecture, and amplifier design to improve laser performance.

01

High-Power Fiber Lasers

Power scaling technologies for kilowatt to multi-kilowatt fiber lasers, including tandem pumping schemes, fiber structure optimization, and thermal management.

  • Bidirectional tandem pumping
  • Confined-doped fiber design
  • High-brightness output
02

Transverse Mode Instability

Investigating the physical mechanisms of transverse mode instability and validating effective mitigation strategies for high-power operation.

  • Static mode degradation
  • Dynamic TMI suppression
  • Low-NA fiber design
03

Fiber Amplifier Systems

High-performance fiber amplifier systems with optimized nonlinear effect suppression for high-power, high beam quality output.

  • MOPA structure design
  • SRS/SBS suppression
  • Narrow-linewidth amplifiers
04

Random Raman Fiber Lasers

Cavity structure optimization for random distributed feedback Raman fiber lasers to improve Stokes conversion efficiency and output power.

  • Full-open cavity RRFL
  • High-order Stokes suppression
  • Power scaling
